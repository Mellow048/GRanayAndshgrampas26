An octopus on a rock. Every arm is a ten-joint chain resolved each frame with FABRIK, a uniform-curvature prior and a tapered travelling muscle wave. The arms are a real kinematic solve: a backward pass pins the tip on the target, a forward pass re-pins the base on the mantle, a curvature prior keeps the bend even along the arm, a phase-delayed wave travels down it, and a final clamp guarantees no joint kinks past 44°. Reeling the crab to the mouth is a deep coil, where reaching passes are ill-conditioned, so that regime is re-seeded from a closed-form arc each frame and rippled at reduced amplitude instead. Residual tip error, clamp count and fold-repair count are measured live above, so you can watch the solver strain when the crab walks out of reach.
